🎵 Add Your Wedding Music Here!

This folder is for your MP3 music files.

Steps:
1. Copy your MP3 files here (1-2 minute songs recommended)
2. Update src/config.json with song titles and file names
3. Songs will play randomly on page load!

Example file names:
- song1.mp3
- song2.mp3
- romantic-melody.mp3
- wedding-theme.mp3

See MUSIC_SETUP_GUIDE.md in the root folder for detailed instructions.
